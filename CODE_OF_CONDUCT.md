# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Discuss code and evidence rather than people.

No harassment, discrimination, impersonation, spam or publication of personal information. Never pressure someone into a call test, ask for their credentials or publish their conversation. Disclose your affiliation when recommending a service.

Maintainers may edit or remove material that exposes private information, lock discussions or restrict participation for repeated violations. A report involving private or security-sensitive details should request a private channel without including those details publicly. See SECURITY.md.
